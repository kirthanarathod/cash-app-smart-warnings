# Cash App Smart Warnings
### Rethinking Scam Warnings to Make Payments Safer Without Slowing Them Down

**Independent Product Management Case Study | Fintech | Trust & Safety | Product Strategy**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**How can digital payment platforms make payments safer without making them slower?**

Digital payment platforms face a critical product challenge: protecting customers from scams while maintaining a fast and convenient payment experience.

This case study explores **Cash App Smart Warnings**, a proposed risk-based warning experience designed to help users recognize suspicious payments and make more informed decisions before sending money.

The concept builds on Cash App's existing fraud detection capabilities rather than proposing an entirely new detection system.

**Project Type:** Independent Conceptual Product Management Case Study  
**Industry:** Fintech / Digital Payments  
**Focus:** Trust & Safety, User Experience, Product Strategy  
**Status:** Conceptual — Not Implemented

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Scammers frequently exploit urgency, emotional pressure, and trust to convince people to send money.

Even when a payment platform identifies suspicious activity, users may not fully understand why a payment is risky.

The challenge is to design warnings that:

- Communicate risks clearly
- Encourage safer payment decisions
- Reduce preventable financial losses
- Minimize unnecessary friction for legitimate transactions

### Product Hypothesis

If Cash App introduces contextual, risk-based payment warnings, then confirmed scam payments completed after a warning will decrease without reducing legitimate payment completion by more than 1 percentage point.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

**Cash App Smart Warnings** introduces contextual payment warnings and risk-based interventions.

| Risk Level | Proposed Experience |
|------------|---------------------|
| Low | Standard payment experience |
| Medium | Contextual scam warning |
| High | Mandatory 10-minute cooling-off period |
| Critical | Existing payment-blocking protections |

The experience prioritizes user safety while preserving payment convenience wherever possible.

### MVP Features

**1. Payment Context Selection**

Users select the purpose of their payment, allowing the warning experience to provide more relevant information.

**2. Contextual Scam Warnings**

Warnings explain potential scam patterns using clear, understandable language.

**3. High-Risk Payment Hold**

A proposed 10-minute cooling-off period gives users additional time to reconsider suspicious payments.

**4. Payment Confirmation**

Users receive clear information about the payment's status and next steps.

## 📊 Measuring Success

### Primary Metric

**Confirmed Scam Losses per $1 Million in Flagged Payment Attempts**

This metric evaluates whether the proposed experience reduces confirmed financial losses associated with flagged payment attempts.

### Proposed Success Target

Reduce confirmed scam losses by **at least 10%** compared with the control group.

### Key Guardrail

Legitimate payment completion should not decrease by more than **1 percentage point**.

### Additional Metrics

- Warning cancellation rate
- Legitimate payment completion rate
- Payment abandonment rate
- User support contacts
- Warning engagement

These metrics and targets are proposed evaluation criteria, not observed results.

---

## 🧪 Experiment Strategy

The proposed validation approach includes:

1. Internal testing
2. Limited safety pilot
3. Randomized A/B experiment
4. Evaluation of scam-loss reduction and payment completion
5. Gradual rollout based on safety and performance results

The experiment would randomize users into control and treatment groups.

Scam reporting delays and incomplete fraud labels would need to be considered when interpreting results.

**No live experiment was conducted as part of this case study.**

---

## ⚖️ Product Trade-offs

The proposed experience considers several important trade-offs:

**Safety vs. Convenience**  
Stronger interventions may prevent scams but introduce additional payment friction.

**Personalization vs. Complexity**  
Contextual warnings may improve relevance but increase implementation complexity.

**User Control vs. Risk Prevention**  
Users should retain control where appropriate, while higher-risk situations may require stronger safeguards.

**Fraud Prevention vs. False Positives**  
Overly aggressive interventions could negatively affect legitimate users.

## 💭 Key Takeaway

Building safer financial products requires understanding not only how technology detects risk, but also how people make decisions under pressure.

This case study reinforced the importance of combining user empathy, product strategy, security thinking, and measurable outcomes.

**Sometimes, the most valuable product feature is the one that convinces someone not to press Send.**
